## About Me

I am an M.Sc. student at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/), advised by Associate Professor [Shuo Wang](https://infosec.sjtu.edu.cn/DirectoryDetail.aspx?id=181) in the Laboratory of Cryptography and Computer Security (LoCCS). I received my B.Eng. in Information Security from [Nankai University](https://www.nankai.edu.cn/), where I ranked first in my major.

My research focuses on building safe, reliable, and transparent AI systems. I am particularly interested in:

- Security of single-modal and multi-modal large language models
- Multi-agent collaborative systems and their security
- Interpretability of artificial intelligence

## Selected Publications

### How Far Does the Poison Travel? Benchmarking Protocol Exploits Across Long-Horizon Tasks in Multi-Agent Systems

**Xiuming Liu** · First Author · Under Review

- Introduces PROTOECHO, an end-to-end benchmark for protocol risks across A2A, ANP, and BCS.
- Evaluates 975 matched malicious-benign task pairs across five domains and three frontier models.
- Connects local exploitability with multi-agent propagation, task consequences, auditability, and mitigation.
